Who We Are

The Department of Cyber Security focuses on developing skilled cybersecurity professionals with strong technical knowledge and ethical values. Students gain practical knowledge through workshops, seminars, projects, and laboratory training.

Our department was established with a clear purpose: to address the growing demand for cybersecurity talent in India and globally. We combine rigorous academic coursework with industry-level practical training to ensure our graduates are job-ready from day one.

With state-of-the-art labs, dedicated faculty, and strong industry partnerships, we provide an environment where curiosity meets technical expertise, and students grow into ethical cybersecurity professionals.

Our Vision

To be a nationally recognized center of excellence in Cyber Security education, producing technically proficient, ethically grounded professionals who safeguard the digital infrastructure of tomorrow's world.

  • Excellence in academic research and teaching
  • Global recognition in cyber security education
  • Nurturing innovation and entrepreneurship

Our Mission

To impart quality education in Cyber Security through an innovative curriculum, practical laboratory sessions, and industry collaboration — empowering students to meet the evolving demands of the digital security landscape.

  • Delivering industry-aligned education
  • Fostering research and development culture
  • Building ethical security professionals

What Sets Us Apart

We go beyond textbooks to deliver a transformative learning experience rooted in real-world security challenges.

Advanced Security Labs

Three dedicated labs equipped with penetration testing tools, SIEM platforms, and forensic workstations.

Expert Faculty

Highly qualified faculty with specializations in ethical hacking, digital forensics, cloud security, and network defense.

Industry Events

Regular workshops, seminars, CTF competitions, and guest lectures from top cybersecurity professionals.

Placement Support

Dedicated placement training, mock interviews, and industry connections to help students launch successful careers.

Research Culture

Students are encouraged to publish research, participate in national competitions, and contribute to open-source projects.

Ethical Foundation

We build not just technical skills but a strong ethical compass — producing responsible security professionals.
